UI/UX DESIGN

The home page of the Petrus restaurant website was created to capture the refined, luxurious, and slightly vintage ambiance that defines the restaurant. With Petrus known for its prestige and carefully crafted atmosphere, the design focuses on elegance, warmth, and sophistication.

A dark charcoal background combined with gold accents sets an exclusive and timeless tone, while clean typography and a polished layout reinforce the high-end aesthetic. Vibrant, high-quality photographs act as the main visual elements, standing out against the muted background and highlighting the richness of the restaurant’s culinary and bar offerings.

The page structure is simple and intuitive, presenting key sections—Restaurant, Food, and Bar—through balanced compositions of imagery and concise descriptions. This approach ensures users immediately understand the identity of Petrus and feel invited into its distinctive world.

Overall, the design successfully communicates Petrus’ blend of modern refinement and classic charm, strengthening the brand identity and appealing to guests by showcasing the unique dining experience the restaurant offers.
